# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes 9,994 retail transactions from the Sample Superstore dataset to identify sales, profitability, product, regional, and customer-segment trends. The analysis combines Python, SQL, Excel, and Tableau to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit trends over time.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Compare regional and customer-segment performance.
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

## Tech Stack

- Python (Pandas)
- SQL (MySQL)
- Microsoft Excel
- Tableau
- Git & GitHub

## Dataset

**Sample Superstore Dataset** containing 9,994 retail transaction records.

## Analysis Performed

### Python
- Cleaned and validated transaction data.
- Handled data types and data quality issues.
- Prepared an analysis-ready dataset.

### SQL
- Analyzed monthly sales and profit trends.
- Identified top 10 products by revenue.
- Compared regional and segment performance.
- Analyzed discount impact on profitability.
- Evaluated year-over-year sales trends.

### Excel
- Created summary tables and product-level aggregations.
- Used PivotTables for sales and profit analysis.

### Tableau
Built an interactive dashboard covering:

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Segment
- Top 10 Products

## Key Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales among the three categories.
- The West region recorded the highest overall sales.
- The Consumer segment generated the highest profit.
- Sales peaked during November.
- The top 10 products contributed approximately 10.65% of total revenue.
- Orders with discounts above 40% averaged a loss of $106.71 per order, highlighting a strong negative relationship between heavy discounting and profitability.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing Technology products.
- Investigate the factors driving West-region performance and replicate successful strategies in weaker regions.
- Focus retention and loyalty initiatives on the Consumer segment.
- Plan inventory and staffing around the November sales peak.
- Protect high-revenue products from stockouts and explore cross-selling opportunities.
